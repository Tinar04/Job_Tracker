About JobTracker
Built during placement season, for placement season.

Why I built this 🔥

During placement season I was applying to 50+ companies and had no idea which ones I'd heard back from, which ones rejected me, or which interviews were coming up. I was maintaining a messy Excel sheet that kept breaking.

So I built JobTracker — a clean, simple app to track every application from first apply to final offer.
